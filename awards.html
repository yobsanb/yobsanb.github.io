Scholarships & research funding

  • 2025 — present

    Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Network · TIRAMISU

    Doctoral research funding through the European Horizon MSCA TIRAMISU network at the University of Cyprus and KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence.

  • 2024

    Monash Graduate Scholarship (MGS)

    Awarded by Monash University in October 2024 for doctoral study in Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering. The scholarship provides a living stipend.

  • 2024

    Monash International Tuition Scholarship (MITS)

    Awarded alongside the Monash Graduate Scholarship in October 2024, covering international course fees and single-cover Overseas Student Health Cover.

  • 2023

    Chinese Government Scholarship · UESTC Doctoral Offer

    China Scholarship Council full scholarship offered with doctoral admission in Software Engineering at UESTC in July 2023, covering tuition, lodging, medical insurance, and a living allowance.

  • 2023

    SUSTech Scholarship · Stipend & Tuition Waiver

    Awarded by Southern University of Science and Technology in June 2023 for doctoral study in Intelligent Manufacturing and Robotics, with a stipend and full tuition waiver.

  • 2021

    UESTC Full University Scholarship

    Full scholarship for master’s study in Software Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, covering tuition, on-campus accommodation, medical insurance, and a living allowance.

  • 2021

    Hohai University Second Class Scholarship

    Scholarship offered with master’s admission in Computer Science and Technology in June 2021, covering tuition and on-campus accommodation, subject to annual review.

  • 2017

    Ethiopian Betre-Science Scholarship

    Selected under the Ethiopian Ministry of Science and Technology’s Betre-Science programme and awarded an Ethiopian government scholarship for undergraduate study in Software Engineering at UESTC, China.

  • Master’s offer

    Zhejiang Sci-Tech University President Scholarship

    Conditional scholarship offer accompanying pre-admission for master’s study, covering registration, tuition, accommodation, a living allowance, and comprehensive medical insurance.
