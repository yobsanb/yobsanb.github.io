Projects

Evolutionary multiobjective neural architecture search

A curated research collection covering evolutionary methods for discovering neural architectures that balance predictive performance with latency, energy, memory, and model complexity. It connects research papers with code, benchmarks, and deployment considerations.

Telecom bandwidth forecasting and anomaly detection

Time-series models for forecasting data-centre traffic and identifying unusual bandwidth patterns. LSTM forecasters use recent traffic history to predict future samples, while an LSTM autoencoder flags windows with unusually high reconstruction error.

Telecom alarm classification

A language-model pipeline for organizing short Chinese telecom alarm reports into meaningful categories. It first discovers groups through sentence embeddings and clustering, then trains a BERT classifier to assign new alarms to those groups.

5G-V2X roadside perception system

A roadside perception pipeline that detects and tracks road users, maps detections into road coordinates, estimates collision risk, and shares scene information with nearby vehicles. It brings vision, tracking, and communication together in an edge system.

Visualizing Gaussian process regression

A notebook exploration of how covariance kernels shape Gaussian process predictions on weather observations. Visualizations compare predicted temperatures with measurements and show the uncertainty associated with each prediction.
