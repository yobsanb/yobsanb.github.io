BMVC 2026

LiDSeg: Linear DehazeFormer Guidance for Efficient Semantic Segmentation in Adverse Weather Conditions

Yobsan Bayisa Leta, Christos Kyrkou, Maria K. Michael, Theocharis Theocharides

British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC) · In press · 2026

Paper record Code
Research summary

LiDSeg adds a lightweight guidance encoder to semantic segmentation models for adverse weather. Auxiliary image reconstruction supports training; at inference, the reconstruction decoder is removed while the guidance encoder and gated fusion remain. The framework supports multiple segmentation backbones and evaluation across fog, rain, snow, and night conditions.
