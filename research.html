BMVC 2026

LiDSeg: Linear DehazeFormer Guidance for Efficient Semantic Segmentation in Adverse Weather Conditions

Yobsan Bayisa Leta, Christos Kyrkou, Maria K. Michael, Theocharis Theocharides

British Machine Vision Conference (BMVC) · In press · 2026

Paper record Code
Research summary

LiDSeg adds a lightweight guidance encoder to semantic segmentation models for adverse weather. Auxiliary image reconstruction supports training; at inference, the reconstruction decoder is removed while the guidance encoder and gated fusion remain. The framework supports multiple segmentation backbones and evaluation across fog, rain, snow, and night conditions.

Telecom bandwidth forecasting and anomaly detection

Time-series models for forecasting data-centre traffic and identifying unusual bandwidth patterns. LSTM forecasters use recent traffic history to predict future samples, while an LSTM autoencoder flags windows with unusually high reconstruction error.

Telecom alarm classification

A language-model pipeline for organizing short Chinese telecom alarm reports into meaningful categories. It first discovers groups through sentence embeddings and clustering, then trains a BERT classifier to assign new alarms to those groups.
